1. The Problem and the Solution

Fraud analysts today have to do too many manual steps for every single alert:

1. Check Transaction Data

Look up the amount, store name, city, time, IP address, and proxy status.

2. Check Customer History

Look at what the customer normally spends and where they usually shop.

3. Search Policy Documents

Read through long PDF policy files to find the exact rules and limits.

4. Make a Decision

Decide whether to let the transaction pass, review it, or freeze the card.

Our Goal: Replace these separate manual steps with one simple screen that does the heavy lifting in seconds.

What Our App Gives the Analyst:

  • Complete Overview: Shows transaction details and customer profile side by side.
  • Automatic Risk Signals: Highlights red flags like proxy IPs or sudden large amounts.
  • Accurate Risk Score: Calculates an exact score from 0 to 100.
  • Official Policy Citations: Shows the exact rule section and page number from the bank policy.
  • Clear AI Summary: Writes a short, plain-English summary explaining the risk.
  • 1-Click Actions: Buttons to Freeze Card, Notify Customer, or Escalate.

Simple Step-by-Step Flow:

  1. The analyst selects a transaction from the list (like TXN_99812).
  2. The backend reads the transaction and customer history from SQLite.
  3. It checks risk signals: Is the amount unusually high? Is it a proxy IP? Did location change impossibly fast?
  4. ChromaDB searches the policy PDF to find matching bank rules.
  5. The app calculates the risk score (0 to 100) using policy rules.
  6. The AI writes a plain-English summary and displays 1-click action buttons.

Two Quick Examples:

Transaction What Happened Result & Action
TXN_99812 $1,450 purchase in the UK using a Tor proxy IP, just 1 hour after a card swipe in the USA. High Risk (Score: 100)
Action: Immediate Card Freeze
TXN_998112 $42 purchase on Amazon from customer's home country using a normal home internet IP. Low Risk (Score: 15)
Action: Normal Monitoring
Important Note: The 0 to 100 risk score is calculated by math and policy rules. The AI only writes the summary; it does not guess the score.

3. Tools Used to Build the App

We chose simple, dependable, and fast technologies to make sure the app is reliable and easy to run anywhere.

Tool What It Does Why We Used It
Python Main programming language Simple, powerful, and connects AI, data, and web easily.
Flask Web server and API Lightweight and fast at sending data to the browser.
SQLite Local database Stores customer profiles and transactions with zero setup.
ChromaDB Vector database (RAG) Searches the bank policy PDF by topic and meaning.
PyPDF PDF text reader Extracts clean text directly from the policy PDF.
Google Gemini & OpenAI AI language models Writes the clear executive summary from facts and policy.
Python Fallback Offline backup engine Makes sure summaries work even with no internet or API key.
HTML, CSS & JavaScript User interface (UI) Creates a clean, modern, and responsive analyst dashboard.

Why This Tech Stack is Great:

Fast and Local

Runs smoothly on a single laptop without heavy cloud setup.

Zero Hallucinations

RAG connects every answer to the official bank policy text.

100% Reliable

Automatic fallback ensures the app never crashes during a demo.

Human Stays in Control

The app recommends actions, but the analyst makes the final call.

4. How We Rate Alerts (Risk Scoring)

Every transaction receives a Risk Score from 0 to 100 based on clear, transparent rules.

Risk Point Calculation Rules:

Risk Signal Condition Points Added Bank Policy Section
Extreme Amount Anomaly Amount is 10x or more of customer average +25 pts Section 6.2 (Page 5)
Large Amount Anomaly Amount is 5x or more of customer average +18 pts Section 6.2 (Page 5)
Unusual Amount Amount is 2x or more of customer average +10 pts Section 6.2 (Page 5)
High-Value Proxy / Tor IP Proxy IP detected AND amount > $500 +30 pts Section 5.3 (Page 4)
Low-Value Proxy IP Proxy IP detected AND amount ≤ $500 +15 pts Section 5.3 (Page 4)
International Transaction Store country is different from home country +10 pts Section 4.1 (Page 3)
Online Purchase (CNP) Card is not physically present +5 pts Section 4.2 (Page 3)
Impossible Travel Velocity Card swipe in another continent within 2 hours +70 pts Section 4.2 (Page 3)

Three Risk Tiers and Required Actions:

0 – 39 LOW RISK

Normal activity.
Action: Standard monitoring.

40 – 79 MEDIUM RISK

Unusual pattern.
Action: Secondary fraud review.

80 – 100 HIGH RISK

Critical violation.
Action: Immediate card freeze.
